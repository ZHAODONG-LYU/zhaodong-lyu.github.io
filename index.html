About Me

Zhaodong Lyu is a Dual-Degree PhD student at Southeast University and Monash University. He got B.S degree in Communication Engineering at Hunan University, 2021, and M.S degree in Electrical Engineering at Shanghaitech University, 2024.

His current research focuses on FPGA/GPU acceleration for autonomous driving inference and V2X Co-optimization. If you are interested in his research, please feel free to contact him.
